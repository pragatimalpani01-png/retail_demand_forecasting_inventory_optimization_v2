# Analysis notes

The project is built around four questions:

1. What demand patterns repeat by weekday, season and product group?
2. Do simple forecasting baselines perform differently on a later holdout?
3. Which items have fewer days of cover than their supplier lead time?
4. How do inventory suggestions change when lead time or safety-stock assumptions change?

The demo is synthetic. Treat all numbers as examples of the workflow, not business findings. For a real case study, run the analysis on an approved dataset and replace this note with verified findings, forecast metrics and a discussion of exceptions.
