# Project description

## Retail Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization

Retail planning is a balancing act. Too much stock ties up cash and shelf space; too little means missed sales and frustrated customers. The buyer has to make decisions with incomplete information, often across hundreds of products.

This project joins three parts of that decision: how demand changes, how well a simple forecast performs on a later period, and whether current stock is likely to last until replenishment. It includes an interactive dashboard that takes the reader from demand patterns to forecast checks and then to inventory exceptions.

The unusual angle is that it questions whether sales are a fair measure of demand. When a product is unavailable, recorded sales may fall even though shoppers still want it. The project makes this limitation visible and explains what data is needed to handle it properly.

All included figures are generated from synthetic demo data. They demonstrate the method, not results from an actual retailer.
